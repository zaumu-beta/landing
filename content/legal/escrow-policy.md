## A Foundation of Financial Trust

At the heart of every successful creative collaboration lies mutual financial assurance. This Escrow Agreement establishes Zaumu's role as neutral custodian of project funds, creating a protected environment where Brands demonstrate commitment and Creators receive guaranteed payment for approved work. These terms form the financial backbone of our marketplace, designed with equal care for both parties' security under Kenyan law.


## The Escrow Framework

When a Brand initiates a project on our platform, they entrust the full contract amount to Zaumu's designated escrow account held with a specified licensed financial institution. This transfer occurs before any creative work commences, transforming promises into tangible financial security. The funds remain segregated from Zaumu's operational accounts throughout the project lifecycle, held specifically for the benefit of the contracted Creator until all agreed conditions are satisfied.


## Deposit Protocols

Brands authorise Zaumu to collect and hold funds in Kenyan Shillings or other approved currencies, with clear conversion rates applied at the time of deposit should foreign transactions occur. Each deposit triggers immediate notification to all parties, confirming the financial foundation for the collaboration is securely in place. We maintain transparent records of every escrow transaction, available for review through your Zaumu dashboard with bank-level encryption protecting all financial data.


## Milestone Payment Mechanics

As work progresses according to the agreed schedule, the Creator submits deliverables through our platform's structured review system. The Brand then enters a thoughtful evaluation period, typically lasting forty-eight hours, to assess whether the work meets contracted specifications. During this window, constructive feedback may be shared, or approval granted to release the corresponding escrow portion. Zaumu acts as diligent steward of this process, automatically disbursing approved payments within two business days while notifying both parties of the transaction. Should questions arise about deliverables, our system extends the evaluation period to allow for professional dialogue, always with the original funds remaining protected in escrow throughout any dispute resolution.


## Dispute Resolution Safeguards

When disagreements emerge regarding deliverable quality or completion status, the concerned party must formally notify Zaumu through our Resolution Center within the evaluation period. This triggers a protective hold on the disputed funds while initiating our tiered resolution process: First, we facilitate direct negotiation between the parties through our mediated communication tools. If unresolved after seventy-two hours, a Zaumu-appointed creative professional (selected for relevant industry expertise) conducts an impartial review. For complex disputes exceeding KES 500,000, we engage a licensed mediator, with costs shared equally unless the mediator determines otherwise. Throughout this process, the escrow balance remains untouched, ensuring neither party faces financial pressure to accept unreasonable terms. Only upon signed resolution or final arbitration award does Zaumu execute the agreed disbursement instructions.


## Fee Structure and Financial Transparency

Zaumu deducts a service fee of fifteen percent from each successfully completed transaction, clearly itemised in all project contracts and payment receipts. This fee sustains the escrow infrastructure, including: - Banking and payment processing costs - Dispute resolution services - Financial record-keeping compliant with Kenyan tax laws - Continuous security enhancements to protect all transactions


## Exceptional Circumstances

Should a project cancel before commencement, the Brand receives a full escrow refund minus our service fee and any third-party payment processing fees. Mid- project cancellations trigger a proportional release protocol based on work completed and expenses incurred, mediated by Zaumu if parties cannot reach mutual agreement. In rare cases where legal proceedings require escrow funds to be frozen, Zaumu will comply with valid court orders while notifying all parties immediately. We maintain professional indemnity insurance to protect against errors in escrow administration, though exclude liability for: - Banking system failures beyond our control - Currency fluctuation impacts - Delays caused by incomplete or inaccurate payment instructions


## Tax Compliance

As financial intermediary, Zaumu complies with all Kenyan Revenue Authority requirements regarding transaction reporting. We issue withholding tax certificates for Creators as needed, though ultimate tax responsibility remains with each account holder. Brands working with international Creators acknowledge potential cross- border tax implications and warrant they will comply with all applicable regulations.


## Termination and Transition

Upon project completion, any remaining escrow balance is transferred according to the final settlement agreement. Account holders may request escrow fund returns after six months of inactivity, subject to identity verification and anti-money laundering checks. Zaumu reserves the right to transfer long-dormant balances to unclaimed funds registries as required by Kenyan law, always after exhaustive efforts to contact the rightful owner.


## Our Shared Financial Integrity

This escrow system represents Zaumu's commitment to professionalising creative commerce. By participating, you join us in elevating industry standards - where Brands demonstrate serious intent through financial commitment, and Creators receive dignified payment security. Should questions arise about these procedures, our financial services team remains available through the contact channels below.
