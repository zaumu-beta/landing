Every Zaumu campaign is paid in **milestones** — funded up front by the brand, released when work is approved. This guide explains the flow so you know exactly where your money is at each step.

## Why milestones

Two problems kill creator–brand deals: creators doing the work and chasing payment for months, and brands paying up front and never hearing back. Milestones solve both. The brand commits the money before you start, so you know it's real; the brand only releases it when the agreed work lands, so they know what they're paying for.

## The flow

1. **Contract** — you and the brand agree deliverables, amounts and dates. A campaign can be one milestone (a single Reel) or several (concept → shoot → final edit).
2. **Funding** — the brand pays the full campaign amount to Zaumu. Until this happens the campaign shows **Awaiting funding** and you're not expected to start.
3. **Work** — you deliver against the first milestone by uploading files or links on the milestone page.
4. **Review** — the brand has a review window (5 business days by default) to approve or request changes. Silence past the window counts as approval.
5. **Invoice** — you upload your eTIMS invoice for the milestone amount. [How to do that](/resources/etims-invoice-for-a-campaign.html).
6. **Release** — the money for that milestone is released to you, minus Zaumu's 15% service fee, and paid out to your M-Pesa or bank account. Payouts run daily on business days.

Then the next milestone starts.

## Revisions

Each milestone includes the number of revision rounds written in the contract (two is typical). Feedback within those rounds is part of the milestone. If a brand asks for something outside the brief — a new concept, an extra deliverable — you can propose a **change order**, which adds a funded milestone rather than stretching the current one.

## If there's a dispute

If you and the brand can't agree on whether a milestone is complete, either side can raise a dispute from the milestone page. Funds stay held while the Zaumu team reviews the contract, the deliverables and the conversation, and makes a decision within five business days. Work that was delivered to spec is paid.

## If a campaign is cancelled

Milestones that haven't started are refunded to the brand minus payment-processing costs. Milestones already delivered and approved are paid to you. Work in progress on a milestone that's cancelled mid-way is settled by the team based on what was delivered.

## Where to see it all

**Dashboard → Payments** lists every milestone, its status and the receipt for each payout, with the service fee and any withholding tax itemised. Those receipts are what you keep for your own books.
