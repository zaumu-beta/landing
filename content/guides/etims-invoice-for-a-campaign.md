Since 2024, KRA requires businesses in Kenya to support their expenses with electronic tax invoices generated through **eTIMS** (the electronic Tax Invoice Management System). In practice that means the brand's finance team will ask you for an eTIMS invoice before they release payment — and on Zaumu, uploading one is part of completing a milestone.

This guide covers the once-off setup and then the per-campaign steps. It reflects the process as we understand it at the time of writing; KRA changes the portal from time to time, so check [kra.go.ke](https://www.kra.go.ke) if a screen looks different.

## Before you start

You need:

- A **KRA PIN** in your own name (or your company's, if you invoice through one).
- Your iTax login, and your tax obligations up to date.
- The campaign's **contract amount** and the brand's **KRA PIN** — both are on the campaign page on Zaumu under **Contract**.

## One-off: register for eTIMS

Individual creators without VAT registration can use **eTIMS Lite**, which needs no software.

1. Go to the eTIMS taxpayer portal (linked from iTax) and sign in with your KRA PIN.
2. Choose **eTIMS Lite** as your solution type. If you're a sole proprietor, select "Individual".
3. Upload the requested document — for individuals this is your ID — and submit.
4. Approval is usually same-day. You'll get an SMS/email when you can start invoicing.

If you're VAT-registered or invoice at volume, you'll be directed to the eTIMS Client software or an integrated solution instead; the steps below still apply, only the interface differs.

## Per campaign: generate the invoice

1. Open the campaign on Zaumu and go to the milestone that's been **approved**. Note the amount — invoice the **contract amount for that milestone before Zaumu's service fee**, since the brand is paying the full amount and the fee is deducted on our side.
2. Sign in to eTIMS Lite (web) or dial **\*222#** for the USSD version.
3. Choose **Sales invoice** and enter the buyer's details: the brand's registered name and KRA PIN exactly as shown on the Zaumu contract.
4. Add one line item. Use a clear description that matches the contract, e.g. "Content creation — Safaricom Q3 campaign, milestone 2 of 3". Enter the milestone amount.
5. If you're not VAT-registered, the VAT field is exempt/zero — don't add 16%. If you are, eTIMS calculates it.
6. Submit. eTIMS returns an invoice with a **control number** and QR code. Download the PDF.

## Upload it to Zaumu

Back on the milestone page, click **Upload invoice** and attach the PDF. The brand's finance team is notified, the invoice is stored with the campaign, and the milestone moves to **Payment released** once they approve. Your payout follows to your M-Pesa or bank account.

## Common mistakes

- **Wrong buyer PIN.** Invoices to the wrong PIN can't be expensed by the brand and can't be edited — you'd need a credit note and a new invoice. Copy it from the contract.
- **Invoicing the net amount.** Invoice the full milestone amount; Zaumu's 15% fee is itemised separately on your receipt.
- **One invoice for several milestones.** Issue one per milestone so each payment matches a document.
- **Adding VAT when you're not registered.** The brand can't reclaim it and it creates a liability for you.

## Withholding tax

Larger brands are appointed withholding agents and may deduct withholding tax before paying and remit it to KRA on your behalf. When that happens, the brand marks it on the milestone and the withheld amount shows on your Zaumu receipt; you'll also see the credit in your iTax ledger. It's not a Zaumu fee — it's tax you've already paid.

> **Not sure about your tax status?** This guide isn't tax advice. A session with a tax agent costs less than one mis-filed invoice, and KRA's own helpline handles eTIMS questions.
