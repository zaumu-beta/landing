Your profile is what brands see when they search Zaumu, so this guide walks through every section and explains what it's for. You'll need your social accounts to hand and a few pieces of your best work.

## 1. Create your account

Go to the sign-up page, choose **I'm a creator**, and register with your email or phone number. Confirm the code we send you. You can also sign up through a brand invitation link — that connects you to their campaign automatically once your profile is live.

## 2. Fill in the basics

- **Name and headline** — your headline is one line, e.g. "Filmmaker & editor · Kisumu". This is the text brands see in search results.
- **Location** — city and country. Brands often filter by location for shoots and events.
- **Categories** — pick up to three crafts (photography, video, lifestyle, design…). These drive AI matching, so choose the ones you actually want work in.
- **Bio** — two or three sentences about what you make and who you've made it for.

## 3. Connect your social accounts

Under **Accounts**, connect Instagram, TikTok and/or YouTube. Zaumu reads your follower count, engagement rate and recent posts directly from the platforms — you can't type these in, which is exactly why brands trust them.

Connecting takes you to the platform's own login page; we never see your password.

## 4. Build your portfolio

Upload 6–12 pieces of work. Tips that make a difference:

- Lead with your strongest four — they show first everywhere.
- Include at least one piece that looks like brand work.
- Add a one-line caption saying what it was for.

## 5. Set your rate guide

Under **Rates**, give a starting rate for your main deliverables (for example "Instagram Reel from KES 20,000"). This is a guide, not a fixed price — you quote per campaign — but it helps brands with the right budget find you and filters out the ones without it. See [How to price your first brand deal](/resources/pricing-your-first-brand-deal-in-kenya.html) if you're not sure where to start.

## 6. Add payout details

Under **Payments**, add the M-Pesa number or bank account you'd like paid into, and your KRA PIN. You'll need the PIN to issue invoices — see [Issuing an eTIMS invoice for a campaign](/resources/etims-invoice-for-a-campaign.html).

## 7. Get verified

Once accounts are connected and your portfolio has at least six pieces, request verification from your dashboard. The team reviews profiles within two business days. Verified profiles get the green tick and show first in search.

> **Done?** Your dashboard's completeness meter should read 100%. Profiles at 100% are invited to campaigns roughly three times as often as half-finished ones.
