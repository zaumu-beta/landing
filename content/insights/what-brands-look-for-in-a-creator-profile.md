Brands on Zaumu shortlist creators from search results and profile pages. We asked the marketing teams doing the shortlisting what they look at. The answers were consistent, and mostly not about size.

## 1. Can I tell what you do in five seconds?

A headline like "Photographer & visual storyteller · Nairobi" beats "Creative | Dreamer | DM for collabs" every time. Brands search by craft and location. Say yours plainly.

## 2. Does your best work come first?

Your portfolio is judged by the first four tiles. Put your strongest, most recent work there, and make sure at least one piece looks like a brand campaign — even a self-initiated one. Brands are imagining their product in your frame.

## 3. Do the numbers match the story?

Zaumu pulls follower and engagement figures from the platforms, so brands trust them. What they check is whether the numbers make sense together: a big audience with tiny engagement raises questions; a small audience with 8% engagement gets invited.

## 4. Have you delivered before?

Completed campaigns, on-time delivery and brand ratings show on your profile. One finished campaign with a good rating does more for you than a hundred more followers. If you're new, a fast, professional response to your first invite is how you earn that first rating.

## 5. Are you easy to work with?

Brands read your proposal messages. Short, specific, with a clear idea of what you'd make for them — that's the whole game. The creators who get repeat work aren't the biggest; they're the ones who made the brand's job easy.
