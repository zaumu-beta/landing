# Design assets

Extracted directly from `Zaumu Landing Page.psd` (163 layers) with
`psd-tools`, so these are the designer's original pixels — not screenshots
and not re-drawn approximations.

## Composites

Two files are flattened from several PSD layers because the artwork overlaps:

- `hero-art.png` — the photo collage (`upscalemedia-transformed`) with the two
  lime scribbles behind it (`Place Your Image Here`, `… copy`).
- `brands-art.png` — the brand dashboard (`Layer 1`) with the blue paint
  scribble on top (`Vector Smart Object (Double Click to Edit) copy 2`).

The individual pieces are kept alongside them (`hero-collage.png`,
`hero-badge-1/2.png`, `brands-dashboard.png`, `brands-scribble.png`) in case
you want to animate or reposition them separately.

## Known gaps

**Testimonial avatars do not exist.** In the PSD the three `Ellipse 2` layers
are empty black placeholder circles — no photography was ever placed in them.
The faces visible in the Figma version come from a flattened AI mock
(`Gemini_Generated_Image_…`), not from real assets. The page renders initials
in tinted circles instead; swap in real headshots when you have them.

**Brand logos are third-party trademarks.** Nike, Safaricom, Absa, Tusker,
Bolt and Showmax are extracted from the PSD as the designer placed them. Get
permission before going live.

## Two copy discrepancies worth a decision

- The third testimonial is **Brian Okello** in the PSD but *David* Okello in
  the Figma file. The page follows the PSD. Note it gives you two Brians.
- The PSD reads "**One** your profile is complete…" — a typo. The page uses
  "Once".
