Questions, answered

When is the service fee charged?+

Only when a campaign is marked complete and payment is released. It's calculated on the contract amount and shown on the contract before anyone signs, and again on the receipt.

Who pays the fee — the brand or the creator?+

The fee is deducted from the completed transaction, so it comes out of the contract amount the brand funds. Both sides see the exact split before work starts.

What happens if a campaign is cancelled?+

Milestones that haven't started are refunded to the brand minus payment-processing costs. Work that's already been delivered and approved is paid out to the creator.

How do creators get paid?+

M-Pesa or bank transfer, in KES. More markets and payout methods are on the way.

Can I cancel a plan?+

Yes — monthly plans cancel any time and you drop back to pay-as-you-go. Annual plans run to the end of the term.
