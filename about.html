About Zaumu

We're building the place where Africa's creators get paid for their work.

Zaumu is a creator-first marketplace. Creators build one professional profile, brands find the right talent for a campaign, and the whole collaboration — brief, delivery, feedback and payment — happens in one place. No chasing invoices. No disappearing clients.

Why we started

Africa has more working creators than ever — photographers, filmmakers, stylists, designers, and the people who make the content brands actually run. What it hasn't had is infrastructure. Deals happen in DMs. Rates are guessed. Usage rights are given away without anyone noticing. And getting paid means weeks of follow-ups.

We built Zaumu to fix the boring, important parts. A profile that shows brands your real work and real numbers. Campaign tools that keep the brief, the chat and the deliverables together. Milestones that release payment when the work lands — so creators aren't financing brands, and brands know exactly what they're paying for.

We're based in Nairobi and building for the whole continent. If you make things for a living, this is being built for you.

What we stand for

  • Creators get paid. Every time.

    Funds are committed before work starts and released on milestones. Our fee is a flat 15% on completed campaigns — nothing to join, nothing to list.

  • Real work, real numbers.

    Profiles are verified and stats come from the platforms themselves. Brands see who actually delivers, and creators don't compete against inflated follower counts.

  • Built here, for here.

    M-Pesa and local payouts, contracts that make sense in our markets, and a team that's a message away. Global brands are welcome; African creators come first.
